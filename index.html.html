Frequently asked questions

How do I buy a rank?

Open a rank, enter your exact Minecraft username, then add it to your cart or buy it now.

Can I pay right now?

Not on this website. Checkout sends your order as a request in a private Discord ticket. Staff then give you payment instructions and verify payment manually.

When will my rank arrive?

Staff deliver your items manually after they verify your payment in your ticket.

Which server do I join?

Use play.evolutionsmp.fun with port 25598.

Where do I get help?

You can get help in the EvolutionSMP Discord server. Open a ticket there and include your Minecraft username and order reference.
